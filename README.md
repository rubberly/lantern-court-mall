# Lantern Court Mall

A walkable three-level 3D mall (three.js r128): 55 generic shops with 4 products each, a turf Garden Level, a glass-roofed atrium with a holiday tree and food court, stairs and glass elevators between all floors, a top search bar that finds gifts by price, age and interest, animated shoppers, a store directory and a per-floor minimap.

- Desktop: drag to look, WASD or arrow keys to walk, Shift to hurry, click a product, E to call or ride an elevator.
- Phone: left thumb to walk, drag on the right side to look, tap a product or the elevator button.

Static site: `index.html` + `three.min.js`. Deployed on Render from `render.yaml`.
